About

Developer, strategist, tinkerer.

I’m Sadiq Emeran — I build WordPress sites, make them discoverable, and put AI behind the busywork.

How I got here.

I started with WordPress because it gives small teams ownership of their own corner of the web — but a site is only worth building if people can find it.

That question pulled me into SEO: the technical audits, the schema, the Core Web Vitals dashboards. Today I do both — build the site, then make it rank.

And when there’s repetitive work — content briefs, reporting, internal linking — I reach for AI: scripts, APIs and workflows that give the humans their time back.
