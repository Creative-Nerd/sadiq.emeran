Giving is Virtue.

The story behind starting up this NPC is close to each of our hearts and was inspired, collectively, by each of our personal journeys. The nature and combination of these experiences stirred our souls toward focussing on our purpose which is ultimately in gratitude through serving others.

We knew that this was something beyond ourselves and hence looked to partnering and collaborating across organisations and people from communities who shared in this passion.

Along the way we managed to find a few beautiful souls who were willing to align and combine resources to drive this shared vision.
